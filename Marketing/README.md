Premium hand-painted 2D game illustration, crisp cel shading, readable wide 16:9 silhouettes, deep navy/gold/mint palette, clean upper-center logo space. No text, real team marks, logos, watermarks, copyrighted characters, malformed anatomy, or photorealism.

### Logo

> Create a transparent, original arcade baseball wordmark reading exactly “MIRACLE DIAMOND” in bold uppercase athletic lettering. Warm metallic gold with deep navy extrusion, a small mint accent, baseball diamond and comet-star motif. Exact text only; clean edges; no characters, mockup, surrounding rectangle, watermark, real team or league branding.

### Composite

> Preserve the key art and transparent logo, then place the exact MIRACLE DIAMOND logo horizontally in the dark upper third. Keep both faces, the baseball, and action silhouettes unobstructed. ### App icon

### Portrait key art
